# Momentum: The Ultimate AI Operating System

This document is the permanent roadmap outlining the long-term vision of Momentum. Momentum is not an experiment. It is a highly polished, fully autonomous, hyper-intelligent AI operating system layer that can do *anything* a human can do on a computer. 

## 1. Native UI Automation ("Hands and Legs")
**Status:** In Development
- **Cross-Platform OS Perception:** Seamless native UI tree extraction via low-level accessibility APIs (macOS `AXUIElement`, Windows `UIAutomation`). Fast, programmatic, and deterministic `(x,y)` mapping.
- **Physical Execution:** Zero-latency physical mouse clicks and keyboard typing directly injected into the OS event stream.
- **Action-Decision Router:** High-speed LLM loop that evaluates UI trees against intent and fires commands in under 50ms.

## 2. Universal Multimodality
**Status:** Planned
- **Continuous Video & Audio Processing:** Momentum will actively watch YouTube videos with you, scrub through video editing timelines (Premiere/Final Cut), and transcribe/analyze Zoom meetings natively in the background.
- **Micro-Vision Fallback:** Ultra-fast, heavily quantized local vision models (running flawlessly on 8GB RAM) that instantly kick in when the UI tree fails (e.g., custom rendering layers, images, videos).
- **Audio Intelligence:** Deep environmental audio analysis. Momentum will not just hear your voice, it will understand desktop audio streams, music context, and sound cues in software.

## 3. High-End Creative & Development Assistance
**Status:** Planned
- **Game Development & 3D Spatial Understanding:** Momentum will navigate Unreal Engine and Unity. It will perceive depth, 3D coordinate space, node-graphs, and complex timeline editor windows.
- **Video Editing:** Direct automation in NLEs (Non-Linear Editors) like DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro. You speak an intent ("Cut the dead silence out of this clip and color grade it warmer"), and Momentum manipulates the timeline physically.
- **UI/UX Auditing:** Deep aesthetic perception. Momentum will analyze Figma or live websites, understand padding, visual hierarchy, contrast ratios, and automatically implement design improvements in the codebase.

## 4. Advanced Gaming & Reflex Automation
**Status:** Planned
- **Real-Time Gaming Automation:** Momentum will have the visual processing speed and physical reflex times required to play real-time games, manage inventory in RPGs, or act as an active, on-screen co-op partner.
- **Screen-Space Overlays:** Drawing tactical HUDs or information natively over your game/app via macOS CoreGraphics overlays without interrupting your workflow.

## 5. Knowledge DB, Persistent Memory, and RAG
**Status:** Planned
- **The Global Mind Map:** A highly polished, persistent Graph Database that remembers everything. It tracks your projects, preferences, file structures, and past conversations permanently.
- **External Data Sourcing:** Flawlessly scouring the web, local documents, and codebases in the background to build an indexed, searchable knowledge base tailored perfectly to your life.
- **Zero-Context-Loss Switching:** If you close the app for three months, it immediately remembers exactly where you left off when you return.

## 6. Autonomous Skills Learning (Self-Optimization)
**Status:** Planned
- **Dynamic Skill Caching:** Momentum learns from you. If it performs a complex multi-step workflow three times (e.g., "Pull the latest branch, run tests, and deploy"), it automatically caches this sequence into a permanent, compiled "Skill". 
- **Behavioral Evolution:** The agent adapts to your specific UI layouts and workflow patterns over time, requiring zero hand-holding after the first successful completion.

## 7. Intelligent Background Service Layer (The True OS)
**Status:** Planned
- **Task Scheduling & Intervals:** Autonomous workflows that trigger on intervals ("Check my email every 30 minutes, draft responses for the important ones, and summarize the rest").
- **Deep Research Agents:** The ability to spawn invisible background "threads." You ask it to research a complex topic, and it spends 4 hours crawling the web, parsing PDFs, and compiling a master document without ever interrupting your current conversational flow. 
- **System Event Triggers:** Momentum automatically triggers actions based on OS events (e.g., "When I open Xcode, set my focus mode to Do Not Disturb and start my Spotify dev playlist").
